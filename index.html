OUR STORY

Built on Faith, Rooted in Community

Wells of Life Pneuma Ministries exists to reveal Christ and nurture the human spirit. We are a vibrant community of believers in Entebbe, dedicated to creating an atmosphere where the presence of God's Spirit is felt, celebrated, and experienced.

Whether you're exploring faith for the first time or deepening your spiritual journey, you'll find a welcoming family here. We believe in the power of authentic worship, meaningful relationships, and serving our community with compassion.
