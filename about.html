About Wells of Life

Built on faith, rooted in community

Our Story

Wells of Life Pneuma Ministries exists to reveal Christ and nurture the human spirit. We are a vibrant community of believers in Entebbe, Uganda, dedicated to creating an atmosphere where the presence of God's Spirit is felt, celebrated, and experienced by all.

Our church is built on the foundation of authentic faith, genuine community, and a passion for spiritual transformation. We believe that when people encounter God's presence and connect with a loving community, lives are changed, families are restored, and communities are transformed.

Whether you're exploring faith for the first time or deepening your spiritual journey, you'll find a welcoming family here. We believe in the power of authentic worship, meaningful relationships, and serving our community with compassion and integrity.

Our Mission

To reveal Christ and nurture the human spirit through authentic worship, spiritual growth, and compassionate community service.

Our Vision

A thriving community of Spirit-filled believers who live out their faith with integrity, impact their communities with love, and help others discover the transforming power of God's presence.

Our Core Values

✨ Authentic Worship

We believe worship is about genuine encounter with God. We gather to celebrate His presence, honor His name, and experience the transformative power of His Spirit in real, meaningful ways.

🙏 Spiritual Growth

We're committed to helping people grow in their faith. Through teaching, discipleship, prayer, and community, we help believers develop a deeper relationship with God and become more like Christ.

❤️ Compassionate Service

We live out our faith by serving others. We demonstrate God's love through outreach, mercy, justice, and practical help for those in need in our community and beyond.

🤝 Community & Fellowship

No one walks alone. We are a family united by faith, supporting one another through prayer, accountability, encouragement, and genuine relationships rooted in God's love.

📖 Truth & Teaching

We're committed to teaching God's Word faithfully and helping believers understand and apply Scripture to their everyday lives. We believe in the authority and transforming power of the Bible.

🌟 Holy Spirit Empowerment

We believe in the present work and power of the Holy Spirit. We welcome His filling, His gifts, His guidance, and His transformation in our lives and ministry.

What We Believe

✝️

Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ is the Son of God, our Savior and Lord. Salvation comes through faith in Him alone.

📖

Holy Scripture

The Bible is God's authoritative Word. It guides our faith, practice, and understanding of God's will.

🕊️

Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit empowers believers, convicts the world of sin, and produces spiritual fruit in our lives.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Church Community

The church is the body of Christ—believers united to glorify God, grow together, and impact the world.
